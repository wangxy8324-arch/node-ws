Research workflow

From Biological Resource to Scientific Evidence

A reproducible workflow helps researchers connect source material, chemical identity, experimental observations, and independently verifiable conclusions.

01 · Resource Identification

Document taxonomy, geographical origin, collection conditions, voucher specimens, permits, and biodiversity-related requirements.

02 · Sample Preparation

Establish appropriate drying, storage, particle-size, extraction-solvent, and sample-traceability procedures.

03 · Chemical Profiling

Apply chromatographic separation, mass spectrometry, spectroscopy, and complementary methods to characterize constituents.

04 · Compound Isolation

Use activity-guided fractionation or chemical-profile-guided separation, followed by purity assessment and structure elucidation.

05 · Biological Evaluation

Use validated assays, suitable controls, concentration-response experiments, replication, and relevant safety assessments.

06 · Validation & Translation

Confirm results independently, investigate mechanism and exposure, assess reproducibility, and evaluate further development requirements.

Responsible research

Ethics, Safety & Scientific Integrity

🔬

Experimental Rigor

Use appropriate positive and negative controls, validated analytical methods, adequate replication, and statistical analysis planned before data collection.

🛡️

Safety Evaluation

Evaluate toxicity, dose-response relationships, contamination, contraindications, and potential interactions before considering any human application.

🌍

Ethical Sourcing

Respect biodiversity regulations, access and benefit-sharing obligations, traditional knowledge rights, and sustainable harvesting practices.
