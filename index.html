Research workflow

From Biological Resource to Evidence

A reproducible workflow connects sample provenance, chemical identity, experimental observations, and independently verifiable conclusions.

01 · Resource Identification

Document taxonomy, origin, collection conditions, voucher specimens, permits, and biodiversity requirements.

02 · Sample Preparation

Define drying, storage, particle size, extraction conditions, and sample traceability.

03 · Chemical Profiling

Apply chromatographic separation, mass spectrometry, spectroscopy, and complementary analytical techniques.

04 · Compound Isolation

Separate candidate compounds and evaluate purity, identity, and molecular structure.

05 · Biological Evaluation

Use suitable controls, validated assays, concentration-response experiments, replication, and safety assessments.

06 · Validation & Translation

Confirm results independently and investigate mechanism, exposure, reproducibility, and development requirements.

Responsible research

Ethics, Safety & Scientific Integrity

🔬

Experimental Rigor

Use suitable controls, validated analytical methods, adequate replication, and statistical analysis planned before data collection.

🛡️

Safety Evaluation

Evaluate toxicity, dose-response relationships, contamination, contraindications, and interactions before considering human applications.

🌍

Ethical Sourcing

Respect biodiversity regulations, access and benefit-sharing obligations, traditional knowledge rights, and sustainable harvesting practices.

Evidence hierarchy: Traditional use, laboratory experiments, animal studies, and human clinical trials provide different types of evidence. Findings at one level should not automatically be treated as proof at another.
